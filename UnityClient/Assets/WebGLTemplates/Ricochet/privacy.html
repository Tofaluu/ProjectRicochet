Privacy policy

Ricochet, at ricochet.thomasliu.dev. Last updated November 5, 2026.

Ricochet is a small puzzle game made by one developer. It collects only what it needs to run the game, its daily puzzle and its leaderboards. There are no ads, no third-party analytics or trackers, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Playing as a guest

On your first visit the game makes a random player id and saves it in your browser, along with your settings (sound, palette, board style) and your name. The id is how the server knows your daily puzzle results are yours. It isn't linked to who you are.

The server stores, under that id:

  • the name you enter (shown in lobbies and on the daily leaderboards);
  • your daily puzzle results: your best solution, how many moves it took, how long you spent, and when you played;
  • simple usage counts: when you visit, solve a practice board, join a lobby or open the daily puzzle, and whether the game is open (to count how many people are playing). These don't include your IP address or device details.

Lobbies and matches with friends live only in the server's memory and are gone when the lobby closes.

Signing in with Google (optional)

If you sign in, Google tells the game your Google account's id and your email address. The game stores them so your streak and results follow you to every device you sign in on. It doesn't get your password, contacts or anything else from your Google account. Google's sign-in button is only loaded when you choose to sign in; Google's own handling of it is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Where it's kept

Everything is stored on the game's server in the United States (Amazon Web Services). A backup copy of the database is made every day and each backup is deleted after 30 days.

Deleting your data

  • With an account: open your profile in the game and choose Delete account. Your account, results, name and usage counts are deleted from the server at once (and from the backups within 30 days).
  • As a guest: clearing the site's data in your browser removes your player id; the results stored under it can no longer be linked to you. To have them deleted from the server too, contact the developer below.

Children

The game isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them beyond what's above.

Changes and contact
