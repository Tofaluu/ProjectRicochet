Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026.

What we store

The game gives your browser a random player id. Under that id, the server stores the name you enter, your daily puzzle results (best solution, moves, time, date) and simple usage counts, like visits and puzzles opened. No IP addresses or device details.

Signing in with Google

Optional. If you sign in, we store your Google account id and email so your results follow you to other devices. Nothing else from your Google account. See also Google's privacy policy.

What we don't do

No ads, no third-party analytics or trackers, and your data is never sold or shared.

Where it's kept

On the game's server in the United States (Amazon Web Services). Daily backups are deleted after 30 days.

Deleting it and contact

Signed in: choose Delete account in your profile and everything is deleted right away (from backups within 30 days). As a guest, or for any question, email tommyli90201@gmail.com
